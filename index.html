A little about my world.

I’m Melchor John D. Rodriguez, a fourth-year Bachelor of Science in Computer Engineering student with a growing interest in web development.

I enjoy exploring how code works, thinking critically about a challenge, and finding practical ways to solve problems. For me, every new concept is another doorway: something to investigate, experiment with, and turn into a useful experience.
